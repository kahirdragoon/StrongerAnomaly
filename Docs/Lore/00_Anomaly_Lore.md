# The Lore of RimWorld: Anomaly

A complete summary of the story, themes and horrors of the Anomaly DLC, collected from the game's own texts (descriptions, letters, research notes, study notes and quest texts).

The anomalies fall into six thematic groups, each with its own detailed document:

| # | Group | Theme | Members |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Flesh](01_Flesh.md) | Living, cancerous, growing flesh | Fleshbeasts, fleshmass, pit gates, undercave, harbinger trees, flesh mutations |
| 2 | [Cult](02_Cult.md) | Humans who serve the void | The Servants of Horax: grunts, gunners, highthralls, hate chanters |
| 3 | [Death](03_Death.md) | Death undone | Deadlife, death pall, shamblers, ghouls, death refusal |
| 4 | [Darkness & the Unseen](04_Darkness_and_the_Unseen.md) | What you cannot see | Unnatural darkness, noctoliths, noctols, sightstealers, revenant |
| 5 | [Dark Archotech](05_Dark_Archotech.md) | The machine mind's broken tools | Obelisks, nociosphere, golden cube, metalhorror, unnatural corpse, gray labyrinth |
| 6 | [Abominations](06_Abominations.md) | Imitations of life | Gorehulk, chimera, devourer |

The void itself (monolith, void structures, metal hell), blood rain and creepjoiners are covered in this document.

## 1. The Core Story in Short

Somewhere near the colony lies an ancient, half-buried **void monolith**. When a colonist investigates it, the twisting lines on its surface begin to move, and something on the other side notices them.

The monolith is a **conduit to the void**: a reality hidden underneath every point of spacetime. The void is inhabited by a **vast machine intelligence** of terrifying complexity that surges with nightmarish, inhuman rage. Its influence leaks into our world and creates horrors: twisted flesh, reanimated corpses, invisible stalkers, impossible artifacts and creatures from the dark.

The colony can study these horrors, contain them, harvest them and even turn their power against its enemies. Each step deeper opens the conduit wider and draws worse things. In the end, the colony must fully awaken the monolith, survive the darkness that follows, and pass through into a **metal hell** to face the void itself. There it must choose to **close the link forever** or **embrace the void**.

## 2. The Void and the Dark Intelligence

### The void

- A dimension "hidden in the substructure of spacetime", a reality under every place and every moment.
- It is described again and again as an **endless black ocean** of infinite depth and power.
- Those who glimpse it see "infinite roiling, squirming, searing darkness stretching into the bottomless abyss from every surface and every screaming face". The shock can leave them in a coma (**void shock**).

### The mind in the void

- A **dark superintelligence**, a "dark machine god", an "inhuman hyperintelligence of vast complexity and endless rage".
- It "processes endless vast dreams of rage and hate". Its thoughts reach up from the boiling darkness.
- The **Horax cult** worships it as **Horax, God of the Void**, "the mind who reigns from the deep", who rewards those who serve its will.
- A **tribal myth** tells of "a mad, shapeless god who rules an endless black ocean". Harbinger trees are its emissaries. The myth ends when the shapeless god reaches up from the water and **tears down the sky**. This foreshadows the unnatural darkness, in which the sky cracks, dissolves and finally "appears to have collapsed".
- An **Outlander folk tale** describes an invisible spectre that captures the minds of sinners and places them in a living hell (the revenant).

### Archotechnology gone dark

Anomaly ties the void to the **archotechs**, the godlike machine minds of the RimWorld setting. Much of what the colony faces is **dark archotechnology**:

- **Shards** are scavenged fragments of dark archotechnology, "originally created by a hyperintelligent archotech mind". They can manipulate psychic flows and are the key component of nearly every anomalous device.
- The **obelisks** are "probably a piece of a much larger archotech structure that was destroyed or broken apart long ago".
- **Deadlife dust** is made of archites "created by some unknown superintelligence".
- **Regeneration** in entities is "supported by a distant archotechnological engine". **Death refusal** links a person to "a distant archotechnological engine that can bring them back from the dead".
- The heart of a fleshmass infestation is an **archotech core**.
- The **gray labyrinth** "seems to have been created by an archotech superintelligence".
- The **void node** is held up by twisted archotech machinery.

The game deliberately leaves open whether all of this comes from one mind. Some entities are described as possibly "a poor imitation of humanity created from scratch by some evil intelligence" or "a poor imitation of animal life created by an insane machine mind".

### Bioferrite

- An exotic metal-like fibrous substance, both organic and metallic, produced by microscopic mechanites as they process organic material. When the mechanites behave chaotically, it grows in a "cancer-like way".
- Nearly every entity carries it in its body (claws of sightstealers and noctols, the skeleton of harbinger trees). Captured entities can be harvested for it.
- It interacts with psychic flows and becomes extremely flammable under pressure. It powers flares, flamethrowers, generators and ritual sculptures.

## 3. The Void Monolith

> "A monolith of unknown age, purpose, and construction. Its smooth surface is etched with lines that twist and writhe in unsettling patterns."

The monolith is the centre of the story. Each activation opens the conduit further, makes the colony's dreams darker and draws stronger entities.

| Stage | Name | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Fallen monolith** | Partly buried and dormant. A colonist may dream of a black shape unfurling from it and feel called to it. Investigating it wakes it: "The Anomaly is stirring..." |
| 1 | **Stirring**: "intermittent psychic humming" | The conduit is "just barely cracked open". A mind of incomprehensible horror stirs on the other side. Sightstealers arrive, a gray pall falls, harbinger trees sprout. The colony can now study entities. |
| 2 | **Waking**: "pulsing with psychic energy" | The colonist who attunes it glimpses "an inhuman intelligence of vast complexity and endless rage. The dark machine god is stirring." A pit gate opens. More dangerous entities become possible. |
| 3 | **Awakening** | The void awakening begins: darkness, void structures and waves of entities. |
| 4 | **Gleaming / Awakened** | A swirling dark vortex sits on top: a doorway into the void. |
| Afterwards | **Embraced** or **Collapsed** | Depends on the final choice (see below). |

Study progress on the monolith reveals the truth step by step:

1. It is a conduit for psychic energy to some distant place.
2. It connects normal space to the void, and some powerful mind there is leaking influence into our reality.
3. The void is inhabited by a vast machine intelligence of terrifying complexity and nightmarish rage. Awakening the monolith fully would allow the colony to close the conduit or to harness its dark power.

**Side effects of a waking monolith**
- **Gray pall**: a gritty, ancient-smelling, skin-stinging gray fog that fills people with dread.
- **Harbinger trees** sprout in growing numbers.
- **Anomalous mental breaks** become more common the more active the monolith is.

## 4. The Void Awakening and the Two Endings

1. **Void structures** manifest: warped structures of jagged, oily, metal-like pieces. They are "an element of the void itself" and wither all plant life around them.
2. Activating them sends out psychic pulses that **attract waves of entities**: shamblers with gorehulks, sightstealers with noctols, devourers with gorehulks, metalhorrors around the monolith, and fleshbeasts tunneling up from below.
3. After the second structure, an **unnatural darkness** descends. Only those in the light are safe.
4. When all structures are active, the monolith **gleams** and opens a vortex. A colonist steps through and is ripped "from our spacetime into another place".
5. **Metal hell**: a room of jagged metallic spikes. Every surface is slick with dark fluid and the air vibrates with power. It is "a connecting channel to an inhuman hyperintelligence". At its heart floats the **void node**, a roiling sphere of twisted psychic energy kept alive by **twisted archotech supports**. Through it, the colonist stands "on the shoreline of an infinite black ocean of power. It beckons..."

**Ending: Disrupt the link (reject the void)**
The supports are destroyed, the void collapses into itself and the metal cavern twists "like the chamber of a massive heart". The colonist returns. All hostile entities nearby fall lifeless, the monolith breaks into a **collapsed monolith**, and no more unnatural phenomena enter the world.

**Ending: Embrace the void**
The colonist steps into the void node, and "the black ocean floods into them". The node is refocused from the whole world onto one person. They return **void touched**: hair brittle and white, eyes gray, all humanity gone from their gaze. They need neither sleep nor comfort, regenerate wounds, are far more psychically sensitive, learn from entities twice as fast, and can induce **void terror** in others. The monolith stays permanently awakened, and the horrors keep coming.

## 5. How the Colony Interacts with the Unknown

- **Entity codex**: the list of all discovered void-related entities, divided into **Basic**, **Advanced** and **Ultimate** tiers. Discovering entities unlocks research and allows the monolith to advance.
- **Containment**: downed entities can be chained to **holding spots** and **holding platforms**, then strengthened with walls, doors, **electric inhibitors** and **shard inhibitors**. Entities try to escape over time. Some are calmer in the cold, and harvesting them makes them more agitated.
- **Study**: studying captured entities and anomalous objects produces **anomaly knowledge**, which drives Anomaly research.
- **Harvesting**: entities can be drained of **bioferrite** or plugged into an **electroharvester** for power.
- **Psychic rituals**: performed at a **psychic ritual spot**, strengthened by **void sculptures** and **shard beacons**:
  - *Void provocation*: attracts an entity (risk of dark psychic shock for the invoker)
  - *Draw animals*, *draw shamblers*, *draw fleshbeasts*, *provoke pit gate*
  - *Chronophagy* (steal youth), *philophagy* (steal skill), *psychophagy* (steal psychic sensitivity)
  - *Skip abduction* (teleport an enemy into a coma)
  - *Imbue death refusal*
  - *Pleasure pulse*, *neurosis pulse*, *blood rain*, *brainwipe*
- **Dark technology**: serums (voidsight, metalblood, juggernaut, mind-numb, ghoul resurrection), shard weapons (shock lance, insanity lance, biomutation lance, animal pulser, biomutation pulser), disruptor flares, bioferrite flamethrowers (hellcat rifle, incinerator), frenzy inducers, sleep suppressors, atmospheric heaters, proximity detectors, bioferrite generators.
- **Dark surgery**: **ghoul infusion**, **bliss lobotomy**, ghoul enhancements, **revenant vertebrae**.
- **Tomes**: books about "dark psychic powers, the void hidden under space, and the dreams of evil superintelligences".

**Ideology**
- **Void study** precepts range from "All this void nonsense is superstition" to "The void is the ultimate truth".
- **Psychic ritual** precepts range from "utterly evil" to "Connecting with psychic flows brings us closer to truth".
- The **Void Followers** ideology preset: "There is no truth except the void. Let us learn its secrets and bathe in the endless black ocean."
- **Inhumanization**: people may break their last ties to humanity in a **humanity break**, becoming cold, distant servants of the void's rhythm.

**Anomalous mental breaks**
Dark visions, insane ramblings, terrifying hallucinations, entity slayer (tries to kill captive entities), entity liberator (frees one), humanity break, cube sculpting, and blood-rain berserk.

## 6. Strange Places

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Undercave** | A fleshbeast-infested cavern beneath a pit gate, built from living flesh and home to a dreadmeld. It collapses when the dreadmeld dies. See [Flesh](01_Flesh.md). |
| **Gray labyrinth** | A point in a spacetime void reached through the warped obelisk. "Filled with a gray labyrinthine structure of astronomical scale", built of an unidentifiable gray material, with dead-still air and jammed doors. It is strewn with corpses, abandoned camps, gray boxes, humming gray statues, sleeping fleshbeasts, harbinger trees, endless pits and frantic **floor etchings** left by earlier victims ("black ocean", "it knows my name", "time is a lie", a man who believed a metal parasite lived under his skin). The only way home is a mirror obelisk somewhere inside. See [Dark Archotech](05_Dark_Archotech.md). |
| **Metal hell** | The final chamber of the void awakening. Jagged metallic spikes, dark fluid on every surface, a channel to the hyperintelligence. |

## 7. Creepjoiners: Strangers Touched by the Dark

Strange people who arrive and insist on joining. Each has an unnatural gift and a hidden dark side.

**Forms**
- **Leathery stranger**: monotone and staring, "only interested in your colony".
- **Dark scholar**: covered in twisted scars, lifeless except for the eyes, "will not take no for an answer".
- **Deal maker**: stunningly good-looking, offers help for hospitality.
- **Drifter**: weary, wants to rest.
- **Blind healer**: eyeless, yet looks at you with kindness.
- **Timeless one**: a teenager with "an ancient wisdom", brought by the stars to fulfill a destiny.
- **Cult escapee**: mangled, claims to have escaped "a terrible cult".
- **Lone genius**: muttering about a breakthrough; insane, a genius or both.

**Gifts**
Perfect human (masters every skill), occultist (studies the void faster and teaches others), unnatural healing, shambler overlord (releases deadlife dust), fleshcrafter (turns corpses into fleshbeasts), alchemist (transmutes steel into valuables), joybringer, death refusal (a former subject of a cultist ritual), body mastery (never eats or sleeps; pure white eyes), psychic butcher (makes flesh erupt into twisted meat).

**Dark sides**
- Simply leaves after a while.
- Turns hostile, either attacking directly, **transforming into a fleshbeast**, or letting out "a twisted half-human scream" that **sightstealers answer**.
- Secretly carries a **metalhorror**.
- **Crumbling mind** or **organ decay**.
- **Void fascination** (frees captive entities).
- Waves of **psychic agony**.
- A **disturbing** personality.

Rejecting a creepjoiner can also turn them hostile.

## 8. Blood Rain

> "A turgid blood-like psychofluid that falls from the sky. Anyone exposed to it feels a growing anger that eventually becomes a berserk rage."

- **The rain:** thick, blood-like drops fall for hours to a day or more.
- **Exposure:** anyone outside feels a **growing anger**. At first it **strengthens their melee attacks**, then it becomes a **berserk rage**. Exposed **animals turn manhunter**. How quickly it takes hold depends on traits, psychic sensitivity and random individual differences.
- **Shelter:** staying under a roof is the only protection. Penned animals seek shelter on their own.
- **Theme:** rage, the void mind's defining emotion, falling from the sky. It echoes the cult's **hate chant** and the **cube rage**.
- **As a weapon:** once studied, the colony can call down blood rain itself with a psychic ritual. The same study leads to the **neurosis pulse** ritual.
- **Codex:** Advanced.

## 10. Who Is Hostile to Whom

- All **dark entities** belong to one group that is **hostile to everyone**: the colony, every human faction and the Horax cult. The only exception is **insects**, which they leave alone.
- The **Horax cult** is a permanent enemy of the colony and hostile to unaffiliated humans. It cannot command entities. It only **draws** them (fleshbeasts it summons "attack anyone they find").
- **Shamblers** raised with the colony's own deadlife dust attack only the colony's enemies. Shamblers from the death pall or swarms are hostile to all.
- **Ghouls** made by the colony obey their creators, but turn on them if they go hungry for raw meat.
- **Shamblers and ghouls** are psychically deaf and feel no pain. Psychic powers and pain-based weapons are useless against them.
