# Death: Shamblers, Deadlife & the Undying

> "Corpses reanimated by microscopic archites. Shamblers are slow, mindless, and will attack relentlessly. Unless captured, shamblers die from metabolic exhaustion after several days."

Back to the [main lore document](00_Anomaly_Lore.md).

## 1. Lore: Deadlife

**Deadlife** is the name for the dust-like substance that raises the dead.

> "A corpse-gray cloud composed of microscopic self-powered archites created by some unknown superintelligence. When they land on dead flesh, they enter the body and reanimate it to create a shambling imitation of life."

- The archites are **corrupted**, nano-scale, machine-like and **self-powered**. They "hang in the air" and "penetrate and reanimate dead tissue at the cellular level".
- Where they touch a corpse they "induce a chaotic and violent resurrection".
- What rises is **not the person**. It is "a twisted imitation of life": a body moved by archotechnology, without mind, speech or pain.
- The archites **cannot sustain a rotting body for long**. Every shambler burns out and dies again from **metabolic exhaustion**.
- The same archite technology, controlled rather than corrupted, appears in **death refusal**, which can rebuild a living person after death.

## 2. Shambler: Shared Traits

> "Shamblers are corpses which have been reanimated by archotechnology. They attack living creatures relentlessly and their immunity to pain makes them difficult to kill."

**Nature**
- **Mindless:** no speech, dulled consciousness, no ideology, no needs, no mental breaks.
- **Feels no pain:** it can only be stopped by destroying the body.
- **Psychically deaf:** psychic powers and pain-based attacks don't affect it.
- **Immune to toxins and infection.** Cannot bleed to death; its wounds only leak dark blood.
- **Highly tolerant of both cold and heat.**

**Body**
- On rising, **old wounds close and lost legs are restored**. The archites rebuild just enough to walk.
- Looks like the corpse it came from, with wounds and decay visible.
- **Slow.** Its dulled mind makes it sluggish, and it attacks a third slower than a living person.
- **Bites and claws** with teeth and hands. It cannot use weapons or shoot.

**Behaviour**
- **Wanders** and **attacks any living creature it sees**. It doesn't care about factions.
- **Cannot open doors.** Leaves no footprints.
- **Keeps fighting while crawling**, even with its legs destroyed.
- Can't be drafted, tamed or reasoned with.

**Death and capture**
- A downed shambler sometimes dies outright.
- When a shambler dies, it becomes an **ordinary corpse** again, scarred and rotting faster than normal.
- **Expires** after a few days of movement. **Captured shamblers never expire** because chained to a holding platform they can't move.
- Can be studied for anomaly knowledge and harvested for **bioferrite**.

**Lifespan by source**

| Source | How long it lasts |
|---|---|
| Wandering swarm | Several days |
| Shambler assault | Collapses within hours |
| Death pall | About a day |
| Deadlife dust (weapons) | A few hours |

## 3. Shambler Kinds

| Shambler | Description |
|---|---|
| **Shambler swarmer** | The common walking dead: a reanimated civilian or tribal in **ragged, poor-quality clothing**, without headgear. The bulk of every swarm. |
| **Shambler soldier** | A reanimated soldier still in **military armor** (basic or advanced), sometimes with **bionic parts and implanted weapons** from its former life. Gear is badly damaged. Rarer and tougher. |
| **Shambler gorehulk** | A **gorehulk** (see [Abominations](06_Abominations.md)) raised from the dead: a huge, swollen, rearranged humanoid monster that now walks with the horde. "Be careful, the group contains a shambler gorehulk." |
| **Shambler chimera** | A dead **chimera** raised by deadlife. A massive and dangerous member of a horde. |
| **Shambler animals** | "A group of shambling, rotting corpses" of any animal kind. Whole herds can rise together. |
| **Any fallen creature** | Any human or animal corpse, and some entities, can become a shambler. |

**Can become a shambler:** humans, animals, toughspikes, sightstealers, noctols, gorehulks, devourers and chimeras.

**Cannot become a shambler:** fingerspikes, trispikes, bulbfreaks, dreadmelds, metalhorrors, revenants, nociospheres, fleshmass nuclei, unnatural corpses, mechanoids, and anyone who is already a ghoul or shambler.

## 4. Ways the Dead Rise

### Death pall
> "A death-gray cloud has descended on the area... The dead will rise! Burn, bury, destroy, or move corpses indoors to prevent them from resurrecting."

- A gray fog of black dust that "smells ancient and putrid" and "enters every crack and crevice". It lasts one to a few days.
- **Corpses lying outside** for a few hours rise one after another as hostile shamblers.
- **Safe:** corpses inside proper roofed rooms, buried, burned or destroyed.
- Every battlefield, graveyard of unburied raiders and pile of hunted animals becomes a danger.

### Shambler swarm
> "A group of shambling, rotting corpses is approaching. Some inhuman force has reanimated these lifeless bodies and set them to murder. They will now wander through the area. If they see a human, they will attack. If not, they will leave in a few days."

Variants:
- Small groups that "might not notice you if you leave them alone"
- Large swarms
- **Animal swarms**

### Shambler assault
> "Some unnatural force is animating these lifeless corpses. They are intent on attacking your colony. As they exhaust their strange energy, they will begin to collapse within hours."

Unlike swarms, an assault **marches straight on the colony** in scattered groups.

### Draw shamblers (psychic ritual)
"Create a psychic pulse which will draw a distant horde of animated corpses towards your local area." The horde attacks any humans it sees but won't assault the colony directly, so the colony can capture shamblers for study.

### Void awakening
"A horde of shamblers have been attracted by the void structures." Shamblers arrive together with **gorehulks**.

## 5. Deadlife as a Weapon

Once researched, the colony can turn deadlife against its enemies. **Shamblers raised by the colony's dust attack only the colony's enemies.**

| Weapon | Description |
|---|---|
| **Deadlife shell** | A mortar shell "packed with a dust-like substance" that settles on nearby human and animal corpses and raises them. It **explodes when damaged**. |
| **IED deadlife trap** | A pair of deadlife shells hidden in the ground that detonate on touch or bullet impact. Only works if corpses lie nearby. |
| **Deadlife pack** | A single-use pack that launches a capsule of deadlife dust. |
| **Shambler overlord** (creepjoiner gift) | "Their body is riddled with corrupt archites. They can release them at will, creating a cloud of deadlife dust which can reanimate nearby corpses." |

**Unique traits of deadlife dust**
- It works **indoors**.
- It even **tears corpses out of their graves** and raises them.
- Its shamblers last only **a few hours**.

## 6. The Undying: Ghouls and Death Refusal

### Ghouls
> "A person implanted with an archotech shard and twisted by dark psychic influences into a jittering murder machine."

Ghouls are the other kind of **mutant**: living people transformed by the colony itself, not raised from the dead.
- **Feel no pain**, are **psychically deaf**, and **regenerate** their wounds.
- Cannot work, wear clothing or hold weapons. They only fight, with **claws and teeth**.
- **Must eat raw meat** (even corpses). If they starve, the **meat hunger** turns them hostile, "even attacking their creators to sate their hunger".
- Their **constant twitching** disturbs people.
- **Enhancements** (bioferrite prosthetics only ghouls can tolerate):
  - **Ghoul plating**: armor, but slower.
  - **Ghoul barbs**: much more melee damage, but slower.
  - **Adrenal heart**: **ghoul frenzy**, a short burst of incredible speed and attack rate.
  - **Corrosive heart**: **corrosive spray**, acid spewed from the mouth.
  - **Metalblood heart**: a short burst of damage resistance, but more vulnerable to fire.
- **Ghoul resurrection serum**: "a mixture of deadlife dust and advanced biochemicals". Injected into a dead ghoul's corpse, it brings the ghoul back after a resurrection coma of several hours. Deadlife can be tamed.
- **Hostile ghouls** appear as threats: "A vicious humanoid ghoul is attacking! It is intent on inhuman savagery and it cannot feel pain." A transport pod may also crash carrying a ghoul in shock, sometimes **a former member of your crew**.

### Death refusal
> "This person is infused with a seed of nanoscale archotech machines that can recreate life. After they die, the archites will sustain their mind and, when they choose, rebuild their body."

- Self-resurrection a limited number of times, with possible scarring and complications.
- Granted by the **imbue death refusal** ritual, carried by some **creepjoiners**, and given to Horax **gunners and highthralls**, who rise once moments after being killed.

## 7. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **The void and the archotechs:** deadlife archites are "created by some unknown superintelligence". The same technological family as death refusal, regeneration and ghoul resurrection.
- **Abominations:** gorehulks and chimeras are commonly found **as shamblers**, and living gorehulks march with shamblers during the void awakening. See [Abominations](06_Abominations.md).
- **Unnatural corpse:** a corpse that walks, but not through deadlife. It is moved by an archotech shard and can never become a shambler. See [Dark Archotech](05_Dark_Archotech.md).
- **Fleshbeasts** ([Flesh](01_Flesh.md)): only the toughspike can be raised.
- **Darkness entities:** noctols and sightstealers can be raised as shamblers.
- **Ghouls:** a sibling mutant. Their resurrection serum is made from deadlife dust.
- **Horax cult:** cultists don't use deadlife, but their elite carry death refusal.
- **Void monolith:** shambler swarms happen even before the monolith has been investigated. The death pall and larger shambler events follow as it wakes.
