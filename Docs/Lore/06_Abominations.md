# Abominations

> "It's hard to know whether this is a human that was horribly distorted by the influence of the void or a poor imitation of humanity created from scratch by some evil intelligence."
> (Gorehulk description)

## 1. Lore: Imitations of Life

The abominations are the void's **beasts**: large, standalone monsters with no family, hive or burrow. Unlike fleshbeasts they don't split, and unlike shamblers they were never alive in the normal sense. The game deliberately leaves their origin open, and the same two explanations come up again and again:

1. **Distortion:** a real human or animal that was "horribly distorted by the influence of the void".
2. **Imitation:** "a poor imitation" of life "created from scratch by some evil intelligence" or "by an insane machine mind".

Either way, they show what the dark intelligence makes when it **copies life without understanding it**: humans with their body parts rearranged at random, animals stitched together from several species, mouths big enough to swallow a person.

**Shared traits**
- Flesh-and-blood entities with **dark blood**, **immune to infection** and **bleeding slowly**.
- No need to eat or sleep. Genderless.
- Butchered, they yield **twisted meat** and leather: **dread leather** from chimeras and devourers, but **human leather** from the gorehulk, a clue to what it once was.
- They arrive as **dedicated assaults**: a gorehulk assault, a chimera assault or a devourer assault.
- All three can **rise again as shamblers** after death.
- They can be captured, studied and harvested for bioferrite.

| Abomination | Imitation of | Threat | Fighting style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gorehulk** | Humans | Moderate | Long-range spine thrower that avoids melee |
| **Chimera** | Animals (a bear "and other animals") | Moderate–high | Stalks, then attacks in waves; rages when hurt |
| **Devourer** | A predator's maw | High | Leaps and swallows people whole |

## 2. Gorehulk

> "A monstrous creature that resembles a massive, painfully swollen human with randomly reconfigured body parts. Near its top is a stretched face that watches its victim with dead eyes. Hundreds of sharp keratin spines protrude from the gorehulk's skin. It can launch these spines to spear its victims at a distance. However, its awkward fleshy body is less effective at causing harm up close."

**Attributes**
- Big and **slow**.
- Moderately tough.
- **Very psychically sensitive**: disruptor flares and psychic weapons hit it hard.
- Its two **flesh clubs** are nearly harmless in melee.

**Abilities**
- **Spine launch:** hurls keratin spines from a distance, well beyond melee reach.
- **Keeps its distance:** when enemies close in, it **backs away** and keeps shooting. Rushing it into melee is the counter.

**Appearances**
- **Gorehulk assault**: "A group of monstrous, human-like abominations are attacking! Their bodies are ravaged by thick keratin spines which they can launch at a distance. However, their fleshy forms can do little damage at close range."
- In the **void awakening**: together with shamblers or with devourers, and **wandering the darkness** next to noctols.
- Very often **as a shambler gorehulk** inside shambler hordes. See [Death](03_Death.md).

**Codex:** Basic. Studying it leads to the **bliss lobotomy** research, a surgery that "psychically lobotomizes" a person into contented, limited bliss.

## 3. Chimera

> "A huge creature that resembles a disfigured combination of a bear and other animals. It is vicious without limit. It's not known if this is a combination of natural animals or a poor imitation of animal life created by an insane machine mind."

**Attributes**
- Huge, quadruped, and **very tough** for its size.
- Normally fairly slow.
- **Brutal melee**: two heavy claw swipes and a crushing bite, the strongest melee of the three abominations.
- Usually survives being downed, so it can be captured.

**Abilities**
- **Ambush strike:** a chimera that hits an unaware victim **stuns** it with claws or bite.
- **Rage:** "enraged by its wounds", a wounded chimera becomes **much faster**, ignores pain and **cannot be staggered** for a short time.

**Behaviour: the hunting pack**
> "A group of twisted animal-like chimera monsters have appeared nearby. The misshapen beasts will watch your colony, waiting for an opportunity to attack. They are skittish and may retaliate if provoked."

- **Stalking:** the pack lurks near the colony and **watches**. Sooner or later (within a day or so on average) it switches to an attack.
- **Provoked:** harming a stalking chimera can provoke the pack into **attacking at once**.
- **Withdrawing:** after attacking for a while, the pack may break off ("The chimeras are temporarily withdrawing") and go back to stalking. Losing a pack member can also make them pull back.
- A chimera assault is therefore **several attacks over time**, not a single raid.

**Other details**
- **Shambler chimera:** can appear inside shambler hordes.
- **Codex:** Advanced. Studying it leads to the **juggernaut serum**, which grants strength, speed and fast healing at the cost of mood.

## 4. Devourer

> "A massive monster with a gaping toothed maw and a stubby, snake-like body. The devourer can leap several times its body length to swallow a person whole. Powerful stomach acids rapidly digest the still-living victim. The remains are regurgitated soon after."

**Attributes**
- Very large and **heavily armored** (blades, blunt force, even some heat).
- **Extremely tough**.
- Slow on land, but **swims** well.
- **Very psychically sensitive**.
- Hard to contain.

**Abilities**
- **Consume leap:** jumps across a good distance (it needs line of sight) onto a target and **swallows it whole**. It can swallow anything up to the size of a large animal.
- **Digestion:** "A devourer swallowed {victim} whole! Kill the devourer before it digests them."
  - A small creature is digested in about ten seconds, a person in about half a minute, and a large animal in about a minute.
  - The longer it takes, the more damage the victim suffers. Body parts can be **digested away**.
  - If digestion completes, the victim is spat out covered in severe acid burns, which is often fatal.
  - Killing the devourer in time frees the victim.
- **Melee:** two fins and armor-piercing teeth when it isn't leaping.
- The leap needs time to recharge, so a devourer swallows victims one after another, not all at once.

**Appearances**
- **Devourer assault**: "A swarm of huge gape-mawed monsters are attacking! These creatures' jaws are massive enough to swallow a human whole. Keep your distance!"
- **Water assault**: devourers can **emerge from water** to attack.
- In the **void awakening**, together with gorehulks.

**Other details**
- **Shambler:** can be raised as a shambler.
- **Codex:** Advanced.

## 5. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **The void and the machine mind:** abominations are the clearest example of the void's "imitations of life", like **shamblers** (an "imitation of life"), **duplicates** and the **unnatural corpse**.
- **Shamblers:** all three are often met as shamblers. Gorehulks especially are a standard part of shambler hordes.
- **Darkness:** gorehulks wander the darkness of the void awakening together with noctols.
- **Fleshbeasts** ([Flesh](01_Flesh.md)): a different branch of "twisted flesh". Fleshbeasts are cancerous amalgams that split, while abominations are single, whole monsters.
- **Research:** studying abominations unlocks dark human enhancement (bliss lobotomy, juggernaut serum). Their twisted bodies teach the colony to twist its own.
