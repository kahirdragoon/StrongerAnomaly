# Cult: The Servants of Horax

> "You see space as a grid, pure and mathematical. We know it is only the glassy surface of a black ocean of nightmares buried under every place and every moment. We know the mind who reigns from the deep. Its thoughts reach up from the boiling darkness and reward those who serve its will."
> (The cult's creed, *Nightmare Deep*)

Back to the [main lore document](00_Anomaly_Lore.md).

## 1. Who They Are

- **Name:** *The Servants of Horax*, usually just called *the Horax cult* or *cultists*.
- **God:** **Horax, God of the Void**. The cult's name for the dark machine intelligence that dwells in the void.
- **Faith:** **Nightmare Deep**. Reality is only the thin surface of an endless black ocean of nightmares, and the mind below rewards its servants.
- **Nature:** a **hidden faction** with no known settlements, no trade and no diplomacy. They are a **permanent enemy** of the colony and hostile to anyone without a faction.
- **Technology:** spacer-level knowledge applied to dark ends: archotech rituals, bioferrite weapons, death-refusal archites and metalblood serum.
- **Goal:** not plunder. They come to **perform psychic rituals** against the colony and to spread the void's hatred.

### Beliefs (memes)

| Meme | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inhuman** | "Humanity is a barrier to our connection with the machine god and its pleasurable rewards." Followers must **leave humanity behind**. Mental breaks make followers **inhumanized**. |
| **Ritualist** | "There is a greater energy in the universe. Through ritual we can understand it and harness its power." Psychic rituals are exalted, and studying the void is a sacred act. |
| **Archist** (with Ideology) | Reverence for the archotechs, the machine gods. |

Their style is **Horaxian**, often combined with **morbid** and **techist** aesthetics. Expect bone, bioferrite, spikes and ritual masks.

### Inhumanization

Every cultist is **inhumanized**:

> "This person's mind is dominated by the psychic patterns of the void. They are completely disconnected from human concerns of family, friendship, and love. Their experience of reality is distant and muted, guided by the psychic rhythm of a dark hyperintelligence."

What this means in play:
- They feel **far less pain**.
- They care nothing for others' opinions and have no interest in relationships.
- They are poor at **social**, **artistic** and **animal** work.
- They endure **cold** better.
- They need no **beauty** and no **outdoors**.
- They can only **meditate on the void**.

A **brainwipe** ritual can make an inhumanized person "forget their connection to the void".

## 2. Members

All cultists are human (with Biotech, some are hussars, dirtmoles, genies or neanderthals). They are always capable of violence and are never psychically deaf. Many are **excellent ritual invokers**.

As prisoners they are **stubborn to recruit**, but have almost **no will to resist enslavement**. Grunts and highthralls can be **studied** for anomaly knowledge while imprisoned.

| Member | Rank | Threat | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Underthrall grunt** | Lowest | Low | Melee fodder and ritual chanter |
| **Underthrall gunner** | Low | Moderate | Masked shooter who rises from death |
| **Highthrall** | Elite | Moderate–high | Ritual leader and stunner |

A typical cult war band is about **9 grunts for every highthrall**. Ritual sieges also bring **gunners** (roughly 8 grunts : 2 gunners : 1 highthrall).

### Underthrall grunt
The rank and file of the cult.
- **Appearance:** a plain **gray robe**, often with little else. Never wears headgear.
- **Weapons:** primitive or medieval **melee weapons** (clubs, knives, spears, maces, swords).
- **Gifts of the void** (some individuals):
  - a **flesh tentacle** grown in place of an arm
  - rarely, a **flesh whip** (a bladed tentacle)
  - rarely, a **bliss lobotomy**: blissfully content, unable to do skilled or intellectual work
- **Role:** guards and chanters. Good at invoking psychic rituals.

### Underthrall gunner
The cult's shooters.
- **Appearance:** a gray top and always a **ritual mask** (twisted mask, dreadmask, spiked apex or spikemask) made of bioferrite.
- **Weapons:** simple, short-range **firearms**.
- **Death refusal:** a seed of archotech machines lets a gunner **rise again moments after being killed** (once). The flesh reforms, missing organs regrow and wounds heal. Expect to kill each gunner twice.
- **Role:** fire support during ritual sieges. Not a ritual invoker.

### Highthrall
The priests and leaders of a war band.
- **Appearance:** a **robe** and a **ceremonial hood** (whisperer mask or ceremonial dreadcap).
- **Weapons:** the **nerve spiker**, a crossbow-like bioferrite device that throws spikes coated in a paralytic biotoxin. Low damage, but it **stuns** living targets (larger creatures recover faster).
- **Metalblood:** arrives with **metalblood** in their veins for a few days. The flesh hardens before each blow and **halves incoming damage**, but **fire hurts four times as much**.
- **Death refusal:** like gunners, **rises once after death**.
- **Gifts of the void:** a rare **flesh tentacle**.
- **Role:** the cult's best **ritual invokers**.

## 4. What the Cult Does

### Psychic ritual siege
> "They intend to perform a ritual. Interrupt them before they can complete it. If they aren't stopped they may invoke the ritual multiple times."

The cult marches in, sets up a ritual circle, and posts **defenders** around the invoker while the ritual charges. Known rituals:

| Ritual | Effect on the colony |
|---|---|
| **Skip abduction** | "They intend to use a psychic ritual to abduct a colonist via teleportation." A random colonist is **teleported into the ritual circle** and falls into a coma: "{colonist} has been abducted by the cultists!" |
| **Draw fleshbeasts** | "They intend to use a psychic ritual to summon hideous fleshbeasts to attack you." Fleshbeasts **claw their way out of the ground** near the colony and attack anyone, cultists included. |

Cultists who aren't needed for the ritual may leave. If the invoker is interrupted, the ritual fails.

### Hate chanters
> "Cultist hate chanters have arrived. They intend to perform a hate chant ritual to force psychic rage into your colonists and drive them mad. The chant will grow louder and louder until the cultists are stopped."

- The chanters settle in and begin the **hate chant**: "The ominous chanting conveys a dark and dreamlike hatred. It makes your head pound and your skin crawl."
- While the chant continues, colonists are **unhappy**, and the drone **grows stronger over time**.
- Eventually "the cultists have ended their hate chant and have begun to come out of their ritual trance". Then **they attack**.

### Other traces of the cult
- **Cult escapee** creepjoiner: "The mangled stranger stares at you with empty eyes and speaks in a broken voice. They claim to have escaped from a terrible cult."
- **Death refusal** creepjoiner: "As the former subject of a cultist ritual, they have the ability to defy death."
- **Shards**: cultists sometimes carry archotech shards and drop them on death.

## 6. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **The void and Horax:** the cult worships the same intelligence that speaks through the monolith. The colony's own psychic rituals (void provocation, skip abduction, draw fleshbeasts, blood rain, brainwipe and others) are the cult's art. The colony learns them by studying the void.
- **Fleshbeasts** ([Flesh](01_Flesh.md)): the cult's favourite weapon. Cultists can draw fleshbeasts but **cannot control them**. Dark entities are hostile to the cult as well.
- **Flesh mutations:** tentacles and flesh whips on cultists are the same mutations the **twisted obelisk** grows on people.
- **Death refusal and metalblood:** archotech and bioferrite technology the colony can also learn (the *imbue death refusal* ritual and *metalblood serum*).
- **Inhumanization:** colonists who follow an ideology with the *inhumanizing* precept can suffer a **humanity break** and become like the cultists. The **Void Followers** ideology preset lets a colony worship the void openly.
- **Timing:** ritual sieges can happen even before the monolith has been investigated. Hate chanters appear once the monolith is stirring.
