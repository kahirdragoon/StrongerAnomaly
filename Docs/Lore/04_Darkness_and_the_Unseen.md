# Darkness & the Unseen

> "The world is blanketed by a layer of unnatural darkness. From the inky blackness, one can hear the inhuman groaning of deadly creatures. Stay in the light."

> "This creature is psychically twisting the perceptions of humans, animals, and machines in the vicinity in such a way as to seem invisible. The eye receives their image as normal but the mind cannot perceive it."

Back to the [main lore document](00_Anomaly_Lore.md).

This group covers the horrors you **cannot see**:
- **In the dark:** the unnatural darkness, its anchors the noctoliths, its hunters the noctols, and the unseen things that attack anyone outside the light.
- **Unseen in plain sight:** the sightstealer and the revenant, which twist the mind so it cannot perceive them.

Darkness and psychic invisibility are fought the same way: with **light, flares and detection**.

---

## 1. Lore: When the Sky Breaks

A tribal myth tells of "a mad, shapeless god who rules an endless black ocean". The myth ends "after the shapeless god reaches up from the water and **tears down the sky**".

The unnatural darkness is that myth made real. It is a psychic phenomenon in which:
1. "The sky is **cracking and dissolving**, immersing the world in an unnatural darkness. This darkness will deepen and become dangerous over time."
2. Then: "The sky itself appears to have **collapsed**. Stay in the light."

From the blackness come chittering, groaning and claws. **Pillars of jagged, oily metal** fall from the sky and hold the darkness in place. Light is the only refuge: the light of lamps, torches and flares.

The darkness comes in two ways:
- **Unnatural darkness** (event): a mid-game horror that can strike once the monolith is waking.
- **Void awakening**: when the colony awakens the monolith, "the sky darkens and a malevolent hum fills the air". An unnatural darkness descends while void structures manifest. Gorehulks and noctols wander the dark.

## 2. The Unnatural Darkness Event

### Phases
1. **Dimming** (about half a day): "The sky is dimming. Soon it will be hard to see anything that isn't directly lit by lamps or torches. Build well-lit areas to survive. Craft disruptor flare packs to explore the darkness."
2. **Warning**: "The darkness is almost here. Only those in direct light from lamps, torches, or flares will be safe."
3. **The darkness** (roughly a week):
   - "An unnatural darkness is descending. Inhuman things move just outside the light."
   - **Noctoliths** crash down from the sky, up to three of them.
   - **Noctols** attack from the darkness in repeated waves: "The darkness has begun to stir. Inhuman chittering sounds emerge from somewhere within the inky black."
4. **The end**, in one of two ways:
   - **Destroy every noctolith**: "With the destruction of the noctolith, a faint crack appears in the darkness, before being filled with more of the sooty black." When the last one falls, the darkness lifts early.
   - **Survive**: the darkness dissipates on its own, and the pillars "fold into themselves and vanish".
   - Afterwards, colonists feel relief: "Colonists breathe a sigh of relief in the clear air."

### Rules of the dark
- **Things in the darkness** attack **anyone standing in the dark**, again and again, with scratches, bites and cuts, until they reach light: "Something in the darkness has attacked {colonist}! These attacks will continue until they are safely in the light." The attackers are never seen. Someone who has fallen and lies **downed** is no longer attacked.
- **No visitors** come and **no other events** happen while the darkness lasts. The colony is cut off.
- Darkness also makes **sightstealers far harder to spot** (see below).

## 3. Noctolith

> "A twisted pillar of jagged blade-like structures made of dark, oily metal. It pulses with energy, powered by dark archotechnology at its core. If you destroy the pillar, you can recover a shard of dark archotech."

- **Role:** the anchor of the darkness. While noctoliths stand, the darkness holds.
- Falls from the sky at the start of the main phase. "They are terrible to look upon but surely contain valuable technology."
- **Defence:** each time a noctolith is badly damaged, **noctols tunnel up from the ground** around it.
- **Reward:** an **archotech shard** for each destroyed noctolith.
- **Approach:** the colony must "reach them by building well-lit paths".

---

## 4. Noctol

> "A large, chittering creature covered with an oily black carapace and armed with claws formed from bioferrite. While it is deadly in the dark, exposure to light irritates its eyes and flesh, slowing it down considerably."

**Description:** "black, insect-like". Its **glowing eyes** are the only thing visible in the dark.

**Threat:** moderate on its own, deadly in numbers and in darkness.

**Attributes**
- **Extremely fast in darkness.** It is the fastest of all void entities, far faster than a running human.
- **Armored carapace:** moderate protection against blades and bullets, lighter against blunt force.
- **Deadly melee:** two **bioferrite claws** that strike quickly, plus a heavy **bite**.
- Sturdier than a human of the same size.
- Unaffected by toxic pollution.
- Immune to infection; bleeds slowly (dark blood).

**Light exposure (the noctol's weakness)**
- In any lit area, light exposure **builds up within seconds**. In darkness, it fades again, but more slowly.
- As exposure rises, the noctol becomes **slower**, its **attacks come much less often**, and it feels **pain**.
- At extreme exposure, it moves at **half speed** and attacks at **half rate**.
- Light turns a hunter into a crippled, easy target. Lamps, torches and disruptor flares are weapons.

**Other details**
- **Arrives:** in noctol waves during the unnatural darkness, tunneling from damaged noctoliths, wandering the void awakening, and alongside sightstealers.
- **Containment:** fairly hard to contain, and escapes somewhat more often than most entities.
- **Remains:** **dread leather** and **twisted meat**.
- **Shambler:** can be raised as a shambler after death.
- **Codex:** Advanced entity.

## 5. Sightstealer

> "Emaciated and misshapen humanoids. Their arms end in sharp, curled claws formed from bioferrite. Sightstealers are fragile, but use psychic influence to render themselves invisible until they get close to their victims. They are known to emit haunting screams as they gather on their terrible hunts."

**Threat:** low on its own, dangerous as an unseen pack.

**Attributes**
- Fragile and slightly faster than a human.
- Two **bioferrite claws**.
- **Very psychically sensitive**, so disruptor flares hit it especially hard.

**Dark psychic invisibility**
> "This creature is psychically twisting the perceptions of humans, animals, and machines in the vicinity in such a way as to seem invisible. The eye receives their image as normal but the mind cannot perceive it."

- **Revealed only up close:** an awake person must have **direct line of sight** within a short distance. Animals never notice them.
- **Darkness is their ally:** in full darkness, the distance at which a person can spot a sightstealer **shrinks to about a third**. Poor eyesight shrinks it further. People with darkness-adapted genes see them normally.
- **Attacking reveals it.**
- **Fades again:** once no one has seen it for a short while, it turns invisible again.
- **Howls when revealed.** The scream is the first sign most colonists get.

**Pack behaviour**
- Sightstealers gather and **call to each other** while waiting to strike.
- Swarms are foreshadowed by **distant shrieks**: "Your colonists hear inhuman shrieks in the distance. Something is out there." / "Your colonists hear a huge number of distant sightstealers screaming in their twisted rage. They are coming. They might arrive immediately, or days from now."

**Where and when**
- **The very first entity** that comes when the monolith begins to stir.
- Arrives in **sightstealer swarms**, and with **noctols** during the void awakening.
- A hostile **creepjoiner** can scream to call them: "In the distance, you can hear other screams answering the call. They are coming..."

**Other details**
- **Counters:** disruptor flares (reveal, stun and light an area), the **proximity detector** (raises an alarm when invisible creatures come near), and good lighting.
- **Remains:** **dread leather** and **twisted meat**.
- **Shambler:** can be raised as a shambler.
- **Codex:** Basic entity.

## 6. Revenant

> "A spindly figure of desiccated skin stretched over an impossibly tall humanoid frame. The revenant's name comes from its pattern of returning to hypnotize new people each night."

> Outlander folklore: "an invisible spectre that captures the minds of sinners, placing them in a living hell. In the story, the victims can only be returned to life by hunting the creature as it sleeps and killing it."

**Threat:** major, but it never fights directly.

**Attributes**
- **Permanently invisible** (dark psychic invisibility).
- **Extremely durable** and **cannot be staggered**.
- Slow-moving.
- Opens **any door**, ignores traps and slips between people without bumping into them.
- **Never attacks** in the normal sense. Its weapon is hypnosis.
- Very psychically sensitive.

**The hunting cycle**
1. **Wander:** it roams the colony unseen.
2. **Hunt:** it picks a victim and **hypnotizes** them. The victim falls into a near-coma, "rambling about nightmarish creatures while their unblinking eyes stare at invisible horrors".
3. **Sleep:** it **hibernates** somewhere on the same map as its victim.
4. **Escape:** if disturbed while sleeping, it flees and later returns to claim new victims.

**Tracking it**
- It leaves **black smears**: "a black slick on the ground... It is still fresh and wet. An eldritch entity passed by recently."
- **Revealed** by disruptor flares, explosives, EMP, firefoam or fire.
- When wounded, it sheds **revenant flesh chunks**. Analyzing them gives longer tracks and lets colonists **hear and spot** it at close range.

**Killing it**
- Death ends every hypnosis.
- The body dissipates, leaving a **revenant spine**: "a chaotic mesh of sharpened metal spokes, twisted into a warped cage... rough-cast black iron smeared in a dark, slippery fluid". Anyone who looks too closely gets a skull-splitting headache.
- **The spine lives:** "The revenant spine is becoming more active and emitting an ominous hum." Unless it is **smashed**, a new revenant **re-emerges** with a new biosignature. Secured on a holding platform, it re-emerges where it can be captured and studied.

**Uses**
- A modified spine becomes **revenant vertebrae**, an implant that lets a person **turn invisible** for a short time.
- Revenant spines sometimes arrive as **mysterious cargo** that someone is desperate to be rid of.

**Codex:** Advanced entity.

## 7. Light: The Counterplay

| Tool | Effect against the dark |
|---|---|
| **Lamps, torches, lights** | Safe zones where nothing in the dark can attack, and where noctols are weakened. |
| **Disruptor flare pack** | "On impact, the flare burns a capsule of bioferrite, which creates a bright flash and a local psychic disruption." Stuns psychically sensitive creatures, **reveals the invisible**, dazes them, then keeps **lighting a wide area** for a while. |
| **Proximity detector** | "Detects the presence of psychically invisible biosignatures" and raises an alarm. |
| **Fire, explosives, EMP, firefoam** | Reveal revenants. |

## 8. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **The void:** the darkness is the void "rushing into our reality". The same darkness covers the land during the void awakening.
- **Noctoliths and void metal:** both are jagged, oily, blade-like dark metal, the material of the void and of metal hell.
- **Noctols and sightstealers** hunt together (mixed packs during the void awakening). Darkness hides sightstealers and empowers noctols.
- **Gorehulks** ([Abominations](06_Abominations.md)) wander the darkness of the void awakening alongside noctols.
- **Revenants and sightstealers** share the same dark psychic invisibility. Disruptor flares and proximity detectors counter both.
- **Shamblers:** dead noctols and sightstealers can be raised as shamblers.
- **Bioferrite:** noctol and sightstealer claws are made of it, and burning it in disruptor flares is the main weapon against them.
