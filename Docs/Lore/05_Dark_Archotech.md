# Dark Archotech

> "It is an archotechnological device that seems to interact with organic creatures at a distance. It is probably a piece of a much larger archotech structure that was destroyed or broken apart long ago. The machinery at its core is inhumanly complex and frighteningly powerful. Its purpose and method of action remain totally mysterious."
> (Obelisk study notes)

## 1. Lore: The Machine Mind's Broken Tools

The dark intelligence in the void is a **machine** mind, and some of its horrors are **machines** too. These anomalies are not beasts but **devices, relics and constructs of dark archotechnology**: dark metal, archotech shards at their core, and psychic influence reaching out to living minds.

Many seem to be **broken**. The obelisks are "damaged and unstable", fragments of "a much larger archotech structure". Their behaviour is chaotic because they no longer work as intended, if anyone ever knew what that was.

**Shared traits**
- **Rising activity:** most of them grow more active over time and must be **suppressed**, or they reach a dangerous threshold.
- **Study:** studying them reveals their purpose step by step and unlocks ways to **use**, **provoke** or safely **disable** them.
- **Shards:** **archotech shards** are both what powers them and what shuts them down. Several can only be safely disabled by spending shards.
- **Risky to destroy:** attacking them "may unleash unnatural and dangerous phenomena".
- **The mind as target:** they twist bodies, copy people, steal them away, inflict pain or capture minds with obsession.

| Anomaly | What it does | Threat |
|---|---|---|
| **Twisted obelisk** | Transforms flesh | Escalating |
| **Corrupted obelisk** | Duplicates people | Escalating |
| **Warped obelisk** | Abducts people into the gray labyrinth | Escalating |
| **Nociosphere** | Inflicts pain, then unleashes an onslaught | Major |
| **Golden cube** | Captures minds with obsession | Creeping |
| **Metalhorror** | Metal parasite that spreads through the colony | Hidden, then major |
| **Unnatural corpse** | Psychically haunts its living twin | Creeping, then deadly |

## 2. The Obelisks

> "A large metallic pillar that emanates a putrid psychic energy. The pillar hums ominously and appears to be increasing in activity. It's not clear what will happen when it reaches its full capacity."

- **Arrival:** an obelisk **crashes from the sky** near the colony.
- **Activity:** it rises steadily. Colonists can be sent to **suppress** it.
- **Study:** marking it for study reveals its true nature in stages. After the second stage, colonists can deliberately **provoke** it.
- **Destroying it** is possible, but "may unleash unnatural and dangerous phenomena".
- **Full activation:** if activity reaches its limit, the obelisk unleashes its power and **self-destructs**.

### Twisted obelisk (the mutator)
> "It is an archotech machine that transforms flesh creatures into different forms. The device is damaged, so the new forms would be chaotic and unstable - a person or animal so transformed would become something totally unrecognizable."

- **Provoked:** "Induce rapid cellular growth in a person causing them to grow a tentacle or fleshmass organ." The person may grow:
  - a **flesh tentacle** (replaces an arm)
  - a **flesh whip** (bladed tentacle)
  - a **fleshmass stomach** or **fleshmass lung**
  Sometimes the obelisk instead warps a nearby **animal into a fleshbeast**, twists a **tree into a harbinger tree**, or fails.
- **Fully activated:** "It swells with grisly energy as the air crackles around it. It is about to self-destruct in a great explosion, but before it does, it will use its hideous transformative power on any creature it can detect." Creatures are **mutated into fleshbeasts**, and more fleshbeasts burst from the ground.
- **Disabling:** after full study, it can be safely shut down with **two shards**.

### Corrupted obelisk (the duplicator)
> "The device has the power to duplicate intelligent creatures. In theory, it can produce duplicates without limit."

- **Provoked:** "Provoke the obelisk into duplicating a willing person." A **duplicate** appears that "claims to be the real" person and joins the colony. The obelisk can also produce a **hostile duplicate** "approaching with murderous intent", or fail ("Something about them is preventing the obelisk from duplicating them").
- **Duplicate sickness:** "Psychic interference between this person and their duplicate is causing slow mental deterioration." It affects **both copies**, keeps worsening for as long as both live, and is not lethal.
- **Crumbling mind:** in some duplicates, "something has gone wrong in the duplication process". Their mind decays within days and the damage becomes permanent unless it's treated in time.
- **Fully activated:** "it would go into a duplication loop, chaotically pumping out a large number of flawed human duplicates." **Murderous duplicates of the colonists** appear, and the obelisk may explode at any moment.
- **Disabling:** after full study, **two shards**.

### Warped obelisk (the abductor)
> "It creates a bridge to a point in a spacetime void separated from ours along usually inaccessible spatial dimensions. The void plane is filled with a gray labyrinthine structure of astronomical scale."

- **Provoked:** "Transport a willing person to a distant spatial dimension. It may be difficult for them to return."
- **Fully activated:** "All manner of creatures are beginning to disappear and will soon vanish entirely." It **abducts creatures at random**, and the obelisk may explode.
- **Attacking it** "may provoke it to abduct more colonists".
- **The gray labyrinth:**
  - "The walls are an unidentifiable gray material and the air is dead still."
  - Doors are jammed and must be forced open.
  - Its rooms hold corpses, abandoned camps, gray boxes, humming gray statues, sleeping fleshbeasts, animals, harbinger trees, endless pits and **floor etchings** by earlier victims, scrawled in chalk, soot or smeared fluid: "black ocean", "it knows my name", "time is a lie".
  - The only way home is a **mirror obelisk** somewhere inside the maze.
  - "The gray structures seem to have been created by an archotech superintelligence, but there's no way to know their purpose, age, or spatial limits."

## 3. Nociosphere

> "A dark metallic sphere covered with curved and jagged grooves. The sphere emanates sensations of pain. It hurts to be near it. The sphere thrums with a mysterious energy. It appears to be increasing in activity."

**Attributes**
- A massive, **stationary** sphere of metal. **Nearly impervious** to blades, blows and heat.
- Doesn't bleed or burn and feels no pain.
- **Pain field:** anyone nearby feels searing pain, stronger the more active it is.

**The onslaught**
When its activity reaches 100%, it "has begun an onslaught of pain and destruction. It will eventually depart - if you can survive long enough." During the onslaught it:
- **Skips** (teleports) around the area at will
- fires **heatspikes**: rapid bursts of a dozen low-accuracy energy bolts
- launches **fleshmelter bolts**: charged energy bolts that explode in searing heat and start fires
- casts **agony pulses**: shock-waves of pure psychic pain that are overwhelming but fade quickly

**Taming it**
- **Capture:** it can be captured on a holding platform, where its activity can be **suppressed**.
- **Purpose:** study reveals "a single purpose - to inflict pain on those around it".
- **Sending it:** study also teaches how to **send** it: it "will teleport to a remote location of your choice and unleash a barrage of pain on anyone nearby... including allies and other entities". It returns afterwards and needs time before it can be sent again.
- **Instability:** in time it becomes **unstable**. Within days it will no longer respond to suppression. The way to dispose of it is to send it against a large hostile force, after which it **departs permanently**.
- **Destroyed:** it leaves behind valuable resources (shards, steel, plasteel, bioferrite).

**Codex:** Advanced. Studying it leads to **insanity weaponry**.

## 4. Golden Cube

> "A cube that fits snugly in the hand. Golden in color, it is always invitingly warm to the touch, like a trusted pet or a hug from a good friend. Those that look closely are rewarded for their attention by the delightful way light plays across its welcoming surface. The cube seems impervious to most damage."

- **Arrival:** it lands nearby, or arrives as **mysterious cargo** from someone desperate to give it away. Someone in the colony "feels inexplicably drawn to the cube".
- **Interest:** it psychically influences those who interact with it. Their interest grows from **curious** to **fascinated** to **obsessed**. Obsessed people sleep less, work slower and break more easily.
- **Cube sculpting:** obsession drives people into a daze in which they build **cube sculptures** from dirt, stone, sand or scrap "to release the pressure growing in their mind".
- **Cube rage:** destroying their sculptures makes them angry, and enough destroyed sculptures send them **berserk**.
- **Withdrawal:** "Their skin itches and their mind races, craving closeness to the golden cube." Kept away too long, they fall into a **cube coma**.
- **Destroying it:** study reveals that a shard can deactivate it, but "the psychic whiplash from the cube's destruction will send any cube-interested people into a berserk rage. Those in caravans will abandon your colony entirely." Some obsessed people **refuse** to destroy it.
- **Codex:** Advanced. Studying it leads to the **pleasure pulse** ritual.

## 5. Metalhorror

> "A horrific shifting mass of metal filaments, blades, and instruments. This metal is maintained and regenerated by a circulating dark fluid. While dormant, fluid metalhorrors are hidden in a host body. They use their host to infect others, creating more metalhorrors. If detected or endangered, the metalhorror will form a jagged exoskeleton and cut its way out of the host's flesh."

**The parasite**
- It lives inside a host, "taking control of higher reasoning via a network of hair-thin metallic filaments that they spread throughout the body and brain. They control the victim's mind and compel them to infect others."
- It **grows stronger** inside the host over time, from larva to juvenile to mature.
- Infected people show no symptoms. **Infected doctors lie** about the results of surgical inspections.

**How it spreads**
An infected colonist can pass it on through:
- **surgery** they perform
- **meals** they cook
- **feeding** patients
- **sharing a bed**
- a creepjoiner's **unnatural healing**

The original infection may come from before arrival, from an **entity's attack**, from **revenant hypnosis**, or from a **creepjoiner** who carried it in.

**Detection**
- Infected people shed **gray flesh samples**. Analyzing enough of them reveals the metalhorror's biosignature, and a **surgical inspection by a trusted (uninfected) doctor** then confirms who is infected.

**Emergence**
It cuts its way out when:
- it is discovered in a **social interaction**, an **interrogation** or **surgery**
- the **host dies**
- the host is **taken away** from the colony
- sometimes, for no clear reason

"The process is horrifying, but the host generally survives."

**Emerged metalhorror: attributes**
- **Fast**, with bursts of extra "machine speed".
- Two **blade limbs** that hit very hard.
- **Armored** against blades and blows, but it **burns easily** despite being metal.
- More psychically sensitive than a human. Immune to toxins.
- **Hibernation:** if left undisturbed it **hibernates**, and wakes again when disturbed. It is very hard to contain.

**Other details**
- **Void awakening:** "Metalhorrors are appearing near the void monolith."
- **Codex:** Advanced. Studying it leads to **metalblood serum**.

## 6. Unnatural Corpse

> "An odd corpse that feels waxy and warm to the touch. Something is deeply unsettling about it."

**The haunting**
- An **exact duplicate of a living colonist's corpse** appears "as if out of thin air", or arrives as mysterious cargo. Its twin is "deeply unsettled by its presence".
- "An archotech shard at its core psychically links it with" its living twin.
- It **cannot be gotten rid of**: it rebuilds itself if destroyed and teleports any distance to stay near its twin.
- Destroying it makes the twin **collapse in psychic torment**.
- It can't be resurrected ("It simply does not respond"), and it never rises as a shambler.

**The awakening**
- Over time "some horrible consciousness is flickering within it". The twin feels it "growing rapidly stronger... Something awful will happen in the next few days."
- **Awoken corpse:** "The unnatural corpse is rising! It intends to attack {twin}! The corpse will follow {twin} forever no matter how far they go. You must destroy it now."
  - Gets **faster every few seconds**.
  - Takes only a fraction of normal damage.
  - **Regenerates rapidly** until its stored energy is spent.
  - Opens any door and **skips** toward its victim.
  - **Hypnotizes** the victim with its gaze.
  - Defeating it ends the torment.

**Ending it for good**
- Full study reveals how to **remove the shard** safely. The corpse then "began to violently bloat and exploded into gore", and the twin "is feeling better already".

**Codex:** Advanced. Studying it leads to the **death refusal** ritual.

## 7. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **The void monolith and void structures** are made of the same dark, oily, impervious metal and are the largest pieces of dark archotechnology (see the main document).
- **Flesh:** the twisted obelisk is a source of fleshbeasts, flesh mutations and harbinger trees.
- **Imitation:** the corrupted obelisk's duplicates and the unnatural corpse are the archotech's copies of people, next to the abominations' copies of humans and animals.
- **The unseen** ([Darkness & the Unseen](04_Darkness_and_the_Unseen.md)): the revenant can implant metalhorrors during hypnosis, and its spine is black iron smeared in dark fluid, much like a metalhorror's body.
- **The gray labyrinth** links to everything else: sleeping fleshbeasts, harbinger trees, and floor etchings about metal parasites and void-black spheres of suffering.
- **Shards** come from these devices (nociospheres, noctoliths, dreadmelds) and are spent to disable them (obelisks, cube, corpse). Dark archotech feeds on and is fought with itself.
