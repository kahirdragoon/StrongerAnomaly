# Flesh: Fleshbeasts, Fleshmass & Harbinger Trees

> "Hideous creatures composed of masses of cancerous flesh. Some contain multiple nervous systems, allowing them to split into smaller fleshbeasts."

Back to the [main lore document](00_Anomaly_Lore.md).

This group covers everything made of **living, cancerous, growing flesh**: the fleshbeasts, the fleshmass that houses them, the harbinger trees, and the flesh mutations that dark archotech grows on people.

---

## 1. Lore

Fleshbeasts are living tumours given shape. Their bodies are twisted muscle and gristle bloated with tumorous growths. Butchered, they yield **twisted meat**. Many are not single creatures at all but **amalgamations of smaller fleshbeasts** linked by a shared nervous system. Each part can live on its own when the whole is torn apart.

They live **underground**, in caverns built from living flesh, and dig their way up to the surface through **sinkholes and pit burrows**. Their largest form, the **dreadmeld**, shares a **psychic connection** with the flesh that holds its burrow together.

Fleshbeasts are also what living flesh becomes when **dark archotechnology reshapes it**:
- The **twisted obelisk** "transforms flesh creatures into different forms": chaotic, unstable and totally unrecognizable.
- The **biomutation lance** and **biomutation pulser** twist living animals or people into fleshbeasts.
- A **fleshcrafter** can "induce a dark archotech to reconfigure a corpse into a horrendous fleshbeast", which is hostile even to its maker.
- Some **creepjoiners** suddenly transform into fleshbeasts.
- The core of a **fleshmass heart** is a "twisted amalgam of flesh and archotechnology".

When a creature is turned into a fleshbeast, its size decides the result: small creatures become **fingerspikes**, human-sized ones become **trispikes or toughspikes**, and very large ones become **bulbfreaks**.

---

## 2. Shared Traits of All Fleshbeasts

- **Mindless and hostile to everything.** They attack humans, animals, other factions and other entities alike (only insects are left alone).
- **No needs.** They never eat or sleep and have no gender.
- **Immune to infection** and **bleed only slowly**.
- **Burn easily.** Their flesh is more flammable than normal flesh, so fire is their natural enemy.
- **Hard to capture alive.** The amalgam fleshbeasts (trispike, bulbfreak) almost always die when downed instead of collapsing, and the dreadmeld always dies.
- **Split on death.** Killing a large fleshbeast often releases several smaller ones.
- **Leave twisted meat** and dissolve quickly. Some corpses melt into liquefied flesh if not butchered fast.
- **Calmer in the cold.** Contained fleshbeasts are easier to hold in a cold room.
- **Studyable** on a holding platform for anomaly knowledge, and a source of **bioferrite**.
- Each fleshbeast remembers **which fleshbeast it emerged from**.

## 3. Fleshbeast Kinds

| Fleshbeast | Size | Threat | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fingerspike** | Dog-sized | Minor | Fast swarmer |
| **Trispike** | Man-sized | Moderate | Splitter |
| **Toughspike** | Man-sized | Moderate | Armored skirmisher |
| **Bulbfreak** | Huge | Major | Bursting brute |
| **Dreadmeld** | Gargantuan | Boss | Undercave ruler |

### Fingerspike
> "A fleshy dog-sized creature with a single spiked limb. Despite their short legs, fingerspikes can move remarkably fast."

- **Attributes:** small, very fast, fragile.
- **Abilities**
  - **Spike throw:** flings a bone spike at very short range.
  - Stabs with its single spike in melee.
- **Dormancy:** often found **asleep** in caves and the gray labyrinth. A fingerspike wakes when a person comes close or when it's hurt, and waking spreads to sleeping fingerspikes around it ("has disturbed several fingerspikes").
- **On death:** bursts into a splatter of twisted flesh and leaves no corpse.
- **Origin:** usually born from a dying **trispike** or shed by a wounded **dreadmeld**.

### Trispike
> "A man-sized amalgamation of several smaller fleshbeasts. Each of the creature's limbs is capable of surviving independently."

- **Attributes:** average speed, **very fragile** for its size, three spiked limbs.
- **Abilities**
  - **Spike throw** at short range.
  - Stabs with three spikes in melee.
- **On death:** "each limb rips free and attacks". It splits into up to **three fingerspikes**. It almost never survives being downed.
- **Origin:** a common emergent fleshbeast, and what a **bulbfreak** breaks into.

### Toughspike
> "A fleshy man-sized creature with two spiked limbs and an armored carapace. Toughspikes throw hardened keratin spikes to take down victims from a distance."

- **Attributes:** average speed, **armored carapace** that protects against blades, bullets and blows. Sturdier than other small fleshbeasts.
- **Abilities**
  - **Hardened spike throw** with a little more reach and damage than the fingerspike's.
  - Stabs with two spikes in melee.
- **Special:** the only fleshbeast whose corpse can rise again as a **shambler**.
- **On death:** leaves a corpse that **dissolves into liquefied flesh** if not butchered quickly.
- **Origin:** a common emergent fleshbeast, and what a **bulbfreak** breaks into.

### Bulbfreak
> "A horrid oozing amalgamation of many smaller fleshbeasts, all controlled by a linked nervous system. It has two large claws which it uses to tear through its victims. Bulbfreaks are often smelled before they are seen, giving off an overwhelming odor of rancid meat."

- **Attributes:** huge and **slow**. Surprisingly **easy to kill** for its size, and fairly resistant to toxins.
- **Abilities**
  - Thrashes with a mass of tentacles in melee.
- **On death:** "they explode into a horde of smaller fleshbeasts", **four toughspikes or trispikes**. Those trispikes can split again into fingerspikes. Killing one bulbfreak can unleash a dozen creatures.
- **Origin:** rarer in normal fleshbeast groups and much more common alongside a dreadmeld. Very large animals twisted by archotech become bulbfreaks.

### Dreadmeld (boss)
> "A gargantuan amalgamation of dozens of smaller fleshbeasts linked together by a loosely shared central nervous system. It uses its massive claws to dig tunnels through solid rock. Fleshbeast dreadmelds are rarely seen above ground, instead sheltering in caverns built from living flesh. They share a psychic connection with the flesh used to create their burrow."

- **Attributes:** enormous, extremely tough, **very slow**. Supported by **impossible regeneration** that heals almost any wound in a short time. Highly resistant to toxins and only partly psychically sensitive.
- **Abilities**
  - Two massive digging claws that strike twice as hard as other fleshbeast spikes.
  - **Shedding:** the first wound tears loose 1–3 fingerspikes. After that, every heavy blow it takes rips more fleshbeasts free, which leap out of its body.
- **On death:** bursts into a **bulbfreak, a toughspike and a trispike**. It leaves behind **archotech shards** and a large amount of **bioferrite**.
- **Psychic link:** its death shocks the fleshmass that holds the undercave together. The whole cavern starts to **collapse within half a day**.
- **Where:** the heart of the **undercave** beneath a pit gate. It's revealed with the warning "Dreadmeld released".

## 4. Family Tree: How Fleshbeasts Split

```
Dreadmeld
 ├─ while wounded ─► sheds fingerspikes and other fleshbeasts
 └─ on death ─────► Bulbfreak + Toughspike + Trispike

Bulbfreak ── on death ──► 4 × (Toughspike or Trispike)

Trispike ─── on death ──► up to 3 × Fingerspike

Toughspike ─ on death ──► corpse (dissolves; can become a shambler)

Fingerspike ─ on death ──► splatter of twisted flesh
```

## 5. The Fleshmass Ecosystem

Fleshbeasts are part of a larger living organism: **fleshmass**, "a solid mass of twisted flesh" that "throbs and shivers with horrible life".

| Part | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fleshmass heart** | "The primary organ of a fleshmass infestation." It digs up from below, gathers material through roots and then spreads fleshmass "in a spurt of cancer-like growth. It will keep growing until it consumes everything." It grows acid spitters, births fleshbeast defenders, and is **nearly invulnerable** due to ultra-rapid regeneration. |
| **Nerve bundle** | Junction points of the fleshmass nervous system. Destroying them yields **neural lumps**. Studying enough of them reveals how to kill the heart with an electrical "tachycardiac overload". |
| **Fleshmass spitter** | A defensive organ that spits acid over long distances. The acid is a byproduct of toxins filtered from the biomass. |
| **Fleshbulb** | Stores and refines nutrients and glows softly with bioluminescence. |
| **Flesh sack** | A stationary fleshbeast that acts as a digestive organ. It cannot digest non-organic matter, so valuables are sometimes found inside. |
| **Fleshmass** | The living walls. Destroying it triggers a **violent response**: "The fleshmass shudders as it births new fleshbeasts to defend itself." |
| **Fleshmass nucleus** | What remains when the heart dies: "a chaotic lattice of fluid-smeared metal... packed with archotechnology" that "slowly twists and bends under its own power". It keeps growing meat that sometimes erupts violently. Left alone, it **regrows into a new fleshmass heart** with a new biosignature. It can be captured and studied. |

## 6. Where Fleshbeasts Come From

| Source | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fleshbeast attack** | "The ground seems to be giving way. The sounds of writhing flesh and cracking bones can be heard from below." Sinkholes (pit burrows) open and fleshbeasts climb out. |
| **Pit gate** | A massive sinkhole that opens when the monolith wakes (or when provoked by ritual). Fleshbeasts periodically emerge from it. Below lies the **undercave**. |
| **Undercave** | A cavern of living flesh with fleshmass walls, flesh sacks, fleshbulbs, sleeping fingerspikes, many fleshbeasts and a **dreadmeld**. It collapses when the dreadmeld dies. |
| **Pit burrows** | Small, steep, unstable holes that connect to "some unseen cave network deep under the surface". They collapse after a while. |
| **Fleshmass heart** | Births defenders and responds to damage with new fleshbeasts. |
| **Horax cult** | The **draw fleshbeasts** ritual makes fleshbeasts dig upward and emerge near the cult's enemies. |
| **Player rituals** | **Draw fleshbeasts** (emerge near enemies, attack anyone) and **provoke pit gate**. |
| **Distress signal** | A camp overrun by fleshbeasts: corpses, fleshmass, flesh sacks, pit burrows and an **ambush** from a nearby burrow. |
| **Void awakening** | "Beneath the ground, you can hear meaty shrieks and scratching claws. Fleshbeasts are tunneling towards you." |
| **Twisted obelisk** | Mutates animals into fleshbeasts. On full activation, it transforms every creature it can detect, then self-destructs. |
| **Biomutation lance and pulser** | Shard weapons that turn a target, or every animal in the region, into fleshbeasts. |
| **Fleshcrafter creepjoiner** | Reshapes corpses into fleshbeasts. |
| **Creepjoiner transformation** | A creepjoiner can suddenly transform into a hideous fleshbeast. |
| **Gray labyrinth** | Rooms with sleeping fleshbeasts. |

## 7. Flesh That Grows on People

The twisted obelisk can also partially mutate people. Each growth "has its own neural structures and may become dangerous if removed":

- **Flesh tentacle**: replaces an arm and is excellent at manipulation.
- **Flesh whip**: a tentacle tipped with a bony blade that makes an excellent melee weapon.
- **Fleshmass stomach**: painful acid that prevents food poisoning.
- **Fleshmass lung**: constantly regrows, immune to lung rot and asthma.

Horax cultists sometimes carry flesh tentacles and flesh whips.

## 8. Harbinger Trees

> "A gnarled tree that grows a flesh-like covering around a bioferrite skeleton. Harbinger trees are capable of feeding on corpses and raw meat, and will consume those placed nearby. If well fed, the grove will continue to grow."

> Tribal myth: "These trees are the emissaries of a mad, shapeless god who rules an endless black ocean. The myth ends after the shapeless god reaches up from the water and tears down the sky."

- **Nature:** half tree, half flesh. A bioferrite skeleton is wrapped in a flesh-like covering.
- **Feeding:** it **eats corpses and raw meat** placed near it. Fed well, it spreads into a **grove**. A harbinger seed grows a new tree.
- **Harvest:** chopped down, it yields **twisted meat** and **bioferrite**. The stumps are ugly and nearly worthless.
- **Omen of the monolith:** a few sprout when the monolith first stirs, and more appear with each stage it wakes.
- **Other sources:**
  - The **twisted obelisk** can twist an ordinary tree into a harbinger tree.
  - They grow in rooms of the **gray labyrinth**.
  - They can sprout as an event of their own.
- **Codex:** Basic, one of the first anomalies a colony meets.

## 9. Relations to Other Anomalies

- **Twisted obelisk, biomutation and fleshcrafting**: archotech forces that create fleshbeasts from living or dead flesh. See [Dark Archotech](05_Dark_Archotech.md).
- **Harbinger trees** and **fleshmass** share the same nature: living flesh that feeds on organic matter and grows.
- **Abominations:** another branch of twisted flesh, but single whole monsters that never split. See [Abominations](06_Abominations.md).
- **Horax cult**: draws fleshbeasts against its enemies but cannot control them.
- **Shamblers**: toughspikes can be reanimated as shamblers.
- **Void monolith**: waking the monolith opens a **pit gate**. During the awakening, fleshbeasts burrow toward the colony.
- **Metalhorrors**: an infection may be traced back to an attack by an entity, such as a fleshbeast.
